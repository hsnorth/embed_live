Quebec Election

What are the parties' big pledges so far?

Swipe to see each party's pledges →

Parti Québécois

Hold a referendum on independence (after Trump leaves office).

Extend the Charter of the French Language (Bill 101) to CEGEPs.

Cut temporary immigration in half — from 500,000+ to 200,000–250,000 — and lower permanent targets to 35,000/year.

Remove the QST on secondhand and used goods.

Québec Solidaire

A wealth tax on ~4,000 households with a net worth over $25 million.

Create a network of public grocery stores to compete with big players and drive prices down.

Cap profit margins of major retailers.

Invest in public transportation.

Conservative Party of Quebec

Bring nearly all government services online, inspired by Estonia's digital system.

Save $3.45 billion in administrative costs over 5 years.

Cut regulatory requirements by 30% over 4 years ("simplify before digitizing").

End reliance on external consultants by building internal tech expertise.

Integrate AI into government, with mandatory human oversight on high-impact decisions.

Quebec Liberals

100,000 new housing units per year.

Refund 36% of the QST on the purchase of a new house or condo.

Involve the regions in determining their capacity to receive immigrants.

Make reading a national priority.

Impose an immediate moratorium on new rules or regulations.

Coalition Avenir Québec

A total of $9.7 billion in election promises this campaign.

$1 billion for home care.

$304 million to support the regions.

$241 million to build 2,000 affordable housing units for seniors.

A further $8 billion in other pledges "are to come."
